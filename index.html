Research interests

  • Modern Korean history The social and political transformation of the Korean peninsula from the late nineteenth century onward, with particular attention to how ordinary people encountered and negotiated state power.
  • Japanese empire and colonial rule The institutions and everyday practices of imperial administration, and the ways colonial policy was made, contested, and revised on the ground rather than only in the metropole.
  • Transnational and migration history The movement of people, goods, and ideas across the region, and what those circulations bring into view that single-country histories tend to leave out.
  • Memory and the afterlives of empire How the colonial past is remembered, commemorated, and disputed in Korea and Japan today, including in textbooks, courtrooms, and public monuments.
